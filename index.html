The Chronicle

Two hearts, one monument

Every monument begins as a moment, and ours was an October morning at Mehtab Bagh, the moonlight garden across the river. One shared bench, one scarf carried off by the river wind, one conversation that outlasted the sunrise as the Taj rose slowly out of the mist — and somehow, that morning decided the rest of our lives.

Five years and two cities later, we are returning to the river to begin our next chapter — adding our names to the city of the world's most famous promise: that love, built patiently and by hand, outlives everything. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us when we do.
